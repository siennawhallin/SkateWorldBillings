Skate Lessons - New!

You should know that at SkateWorld Billings, we offer lessons for skaters of all ages and skill levels!

Beginner Skate Lessons

  • Perfect for new skaters looking to learn the basics
  • 1 hour of lesson time
  • Focus on balance, turning, and stopping techniques
  • Small class sizes for individual attention

$30 per session

Intermediate Skate Lessons

  • Designed for skaters who want to refine their skills
  • 1.5 hours of lesson time
  • Focus on improving technique, speed, and control
  • Group lessons with personal feedback

$40 per session

Advanced Skate Lessons

  • For skaters looking to master tricks and advanced techniques
  • 2 hours of lesson time
  • Focus on jumps, spins, and advanced maneuvers
  • One-on-one coaching for personalized progression

$50 per session


SkateWorld Billings' Rules & Safety


  • No skating outside the building.
  • Bullying and/or intimidation will not be tolerated.
  • All accidents or injuries must be reported to management.
  • Explicit lyric music will not be played.
  • No reckless or careless behavior.
  • No carrying children while skating.
  • Outside food/drink only with management approval.
  • Cash only accepted in the Snack Bar.
  • Skate Buddy rentals limited to under 60 inches. Not available Saturday nights.
  • No skating under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
